How it works

From first look to finished exterior in a weekend

We've been doing this since 1981. Here's how to go from picking a color to standing back and admiring your work.

1

Order a sample

Get a hand sample and color chip ring shipped to your door for $20-$25. See the texture, feel the weight, and hold it up to your home before committing to a full order.

2

Measure your project

Walk your home and measure the sections you're siding. Write down the length, height, and note your door and window openings. Rough numbers are fine at this stage.

3

Use the project calculator

Enter your measurements. The calculator accounts for waste, outputs the right number of cartons, and adds coordinated corners, trim, and starter strips automatically.

4

Order direct, ship nationwide

Place your order online or by phone. We'll arrange LTL freight shipping to your door. Most orders ship within 5 business days, with some colors shipping in 15.

Project calculator

Not sure how much you need?

Enter your home's dimensions and get a carton count in seconds. Accounts for windows, doors, and a 10% waste buffer. No sign-up required.

About Everlock

Factory direct from Colorado. For over 45 years.

Everlock Home Supply is a division of Everlock Systems, Inc., a family-owned manufacturer based in Boulder, Colorado. Since 1981, we've been helping homeowners, contractors, and DIYers upgrade the exterior of conventional homes, mobile homes, and manufactured homes.

As an exclusive online supplier of Novik polymer siding and roofing, we sell directly from our Colorado facility. No distributor markup. No middleman. When you order from EverLockHomeSupply.com, you're ordering from the people who know these products inside out.
